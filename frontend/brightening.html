Hada Labo Whitening Lotion

PKR 5,000

A cult Japanese favourite, made entirely in Japan.

What it does

  • Brightening: This lotion is a cult Japanese product that people swear by. Tranexamic acid helps reduce the look of dark spots and melasma.
  • Super Hydration: Four types of hyaluronic acid flood the skin with moisture.
  • Smooth Skin: Licorice root helps prevent rough, irritated skin.
Select actives
  • Tranexamic Acid (Active Ingredient): Blocks the pathway that creates dark spots to brighten and even out skin tone.
  • Dipotassium Glycyrrhizate (Active Ingredient): Calms redness and prevents rough skin.
  • Nano Hyaluronic Acid: Tiny hyaluronic acid that hydrates deeper layers of the skin.
  • Super Hyaluronic Acid (Sodium Acetylated Hyaluronate): Holds about twice as much moisture as regular hyaluronic acid and keeps skin moist longer.
  • Fermented Hyaluronic Acid: Supports the skin's own moisture barrier.
